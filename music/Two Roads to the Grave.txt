(Verse 1)
From rusty cage and dungeon deep,
We're souls the hangman couldn't keep.
No banners bright, no noble call,
Just broken men to watch the fall.

(Chorus)
We march to the General's drum,
For a war that's yet to come.
Obey his word and meet the blade,
Disobey, the debt is paid.
There's no escape, no path to save,
Just two roads to the same cold grave.

(Verse 2)
We're not his soldiers, proud and tall,
We are the ones who take the fall.
The spark to light a burning pyre,
To feed a warmonger's desire.

(Chorus)
We march to the General's drum,
For a war that's yet to come.
Obey his word and meet the blade,
Disobey, the debt is paid.
There's no escape, no path to save,
Just two roads to the same cold grave.

(Bridge)
So let them watch us, steel in hand,
A cursed and condemned band.
We'll face the shadow, face the fire,
Upon this funeral pyre.

(Outro)
Left or right...
It ends in night.
